Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains how Meet GH handles information when adults use our dating and social community. Replace the operator/contact placeholders below with your registered business details and have this policy reviewed for applicable Ghanaian data-protection requirements before public launch.

1. Information we collect

We collect account details such as email, first name, date of birth, city, profile biography, interests and profile photos. We calculate your age from your date of birth. Your full date of birth is not displayed to other members. For optional selfie review, we collect a private selfie image and verification status. Meet GH does not request government-issued ID documents for this review. Selfie review is not proof of legal identity or age. If you use messaging, calls, posts, reports, location sharing or push alerts, we process the information needed to provide those features.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify that members are adults, display profiles, facilitate matches and communications, prevent fraud and abuse, investigate reports, provide support and improve reliability. Approximate location is optional and is used only when you enable nearby sharing.

3. Identity documents and retention

ID images are stored in restricted private storage and are available only to authorised moderators for verification. After an account is approved, the ID image is scheduled for deletion after 30 days. Deletion depends on the scheduled retention-cleanup job being deployed and monitored. Verification outcomes and audit records may be retained after the image is deleted where necessary for safety, security or legal obligations.

4. Profile content and moderation

Changed profile biographies and photos may be held from public display until reviewed. Reported profiles, posts, images, videos and voice notes may be reviewed by authorised moderators. Moderation decisions are recorded to support safety and accountability.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers that host authentication, databases, storage, push delivery and app services, and may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect members and the service.

6. Security

We use access controls, private media storage and restricted administrator functions. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and report suspicious activity.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit permitted profile information, manage optional location and notification settings, report or block members, and request account deletion in the app. You may contact the service operator to request access, correction or other action available under applicable data-protection law.

8. Children

Meet GH is intended only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow under-18s to use the service. Suspected underage accounts should be reported for prompt review.
